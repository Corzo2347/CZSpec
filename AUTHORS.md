# Authors and contributors

## Original creator

**Oscar Corzo** — creator, original author, and maintainer of CZSpec.

CZSpec was conceived and developed as a scientific desktop application for radioastronomical and molecular spectral-line analysis.

## Contributors

Contributors whose work is accepted into the official CZSpec repository should be credited for the specific code, documentation, scientific method, module, or other contribution they provide.

A contribution does **not** make Oscar Corzo a co-author of that specific contribution when he did not participate in its creation. Likewise, contributors do not become co-authors of unrelated parts of CZSpec. The project keeps both kinds of credit explicit:

- **CZSpec original creator:** Oscar Corzo.
- **Contribution/module author(s):** the person or people who actually created that contribution.

Git history and Pull Requests remain the authoritative record of individual contributions.

## Community modules

Each accepted community module under `mods/` must declare its own author(s), version, compatibility, and license information in its module README and manifest.

A typical attribution should look like:

> **Module:** Rotational Diagrams  
> **Author:** Example Contributor  
> **For:** CZSpec  
> **CZSpec original creator:** Oscar Corzo

## Citation

Using CZSpec does not by itself establish authorship of a scientific paper. If CZSpec contributes to published research, users are strongly requested to cite the software using the metadata in `CITATION.cff`.

Copyright notices and authorship attribution required by the software license must be preserved.
